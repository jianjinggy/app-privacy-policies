1. What this policy covers

This policy applies only to the iOS/iPadOS game Neon Ballway / 霓虹跃球 published or intended for publication by Everyday Game Studio under bundle identifier com.xuzengfei.ballrun4. The game is a casual color-matching and bouncing game with automatically moving levels, horizontal drag controls, scores, stars, language settings, and interstitial advertising. It does not provide accounts, social features, chat, user-generated content, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or payment processing.

2. Information stored by the game

The game stores limited gameplay and settings information in local device storage so that the experience works as expected:

  • the language selected in Settings;
  • local level progress, best scores, and star results; and
  • a local timestamp used to enforce the interval between interstitial ads.

The game code does not require a name, email address, account, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or payment details. These local values are not sent to an Everyday Game Studio server by the game. Deleting the app or using an available iOS storage/reset action removes local records, subject to any operating-system backup behavior.

3. Google Mobile Ads and information that may be processed

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to request and show interstitial ads at eligible level-victory screens. Google and its service providers may process information needed to request, deliver, measure, secure, and limit advertising. Depending on the device, operating system, region, consent choice, and final ad configuration, this may include:

  • IP address and a general or approximate location inferred from network information;
  • device, app, operating-system, network, and advertising identifiers or similar technical identifiers;
  • ad requests, impressions, interactions, frequency-capping events, and diagnostic or performance information; and
  • signals used for fraud prevention, security, aggregated reporting, or ad selection.

The exact processing is controlled in part by Google's SDK and the publisher's AdMob settings. Google may share relevant information with its advertising and measurement partners under its policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

For the current development configuration, the official Google test values are iOS test App ID ca-app-pub-3940256099942544~1458002511 and iOS interstitial test unit ca-app-pub-3940256099942544/4411468910. These are not production advertising identifiers.

4. iOS/iPadOS platform differences

The current iOS/iPadOS app does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, or location permissions. It does include the Google Mobile Ads configuration and an NSUserTrackingUsageDescription text entry. The current app code does not call Apple's AppTrackingTransparency authorization request; the presence of that description alone does not mean that tracking permission has been requested or granted.

If a future release uses tracking or personalized advertising in a way that requires AppTrackingTransparency, the app will present the applicable system permission and consent flow before that use, and the App Store privacy answers and this policy will be updated. Apple may also process data through platform services under Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Consent and advertising choices

The project includes Google Mobile Ads dependencies, but the current app code does not yet complete the app-level Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent-information update and consent-form flow. The production release must complete and test the applicable consent flow before advertising is treated as release-ready. This page records the current development state; it is not a statement that UMP or AppTrackingTransparency integration has already been completed.

Users can review Apple's device privacy and tracking controls and Google's advertising controls at Google Ads Settings. Available choices and ad behavior vary by location, law, device, and the final configuration.

6. Retention and deletion

Local language, progress, score, star, and ad-frequency records remain on the device until the user deletes the app or uses an available iOS storage/reset action. The developer does not operate an account system for this game. Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and controls; the developer does not control Google's retention periods. Privacy questions about developer-controlled information can be sent to the contact address below.

7. Children's privacy

The game does not require an account and does not knowingly ask children or adults to submit personal information directly to the developer. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Before release, the App Store age-rating, child-directed treatment, advertising, and App Privacy settings must be completed and kept consistent with the final product and applicable law. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us so that we can review the request.
