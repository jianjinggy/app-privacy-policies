1. What this policy covers

This policy applies only to the iOS/iPadOS game Neon Ballway / 霓虹跃球 published or intended for publication by Everyday Game Studio under bundle identifier com.xuzengfei.ballrun4. The game is a casual color-matching and bouncing game with automatically moving levels, horizontal drag controls, scores, stars, language settings, and interstitial advertising. It does not provide accounts, social features, chat, user-generated content, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or payment processing.

2. Information stored by the game

The game stores limited gameplay and settings information in local device storage so that the experience works as expected:

  • the language selected in Settings;
  • local level progress, best scores, and star results; and
  • a local timestamp used to enforce the interval between interstitial ads.

The game code does not require a name, email address, account, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or payment details. These local values are not sent to an Everyday Game Studio server by the game. Deleting the app or using an available iOS storage/reset action removes local records, subject to any operating-system backup behavior.

3. Google Mobile Ads and information that may be processed

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to request and show interstitial ads at eligible level-victory screens. The current iOS configuration uses AdMob App ID ca-app-pub-6195112913810746~5402253455 and interstitial ad unit ca-app-pub-6195112913810746/6557116127. Google and its service providers may process information needed to request, deliver, measure, secure, and limit advertising. Depending on the device, operating system, region, and consent choice, this may include:

  • IP address and a general or approximate location inferred from network information;
  • device, app, operating-system, network, and advertising identifiers or similar technical identifiers;
  • ad requests, impressions, interactions, frequency-capping events, and diagnostic or performance information; and
  • signals used for fraud prevention, security, aggregated reporting, or ad selection.

The exact processing is controlled in part by Google's SDK and the publisher's AdMob settings. Google may share relevant information with its advertising and measurement partners under its policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. iOS/iPadOS platform differences

The current iOS/iPadOS app does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, or location permissions. It includes the Google Mobile Ads configuration, an NSUserTrackingUsageDescription text entry, and the ATT calls used by the AdMob plugin. During initialization, the app checks the ATT status and requests authorization only when the status is notDetermined. The app can still run if authorization is denied or restricted; advertising personalization and measurement then depend on Apple's and Google's applicable controls.

Apple may process data through platform services under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app does not request precise location or any other permission not described in this policy.

5. Consent and advertising choices

During initialization on iOS/iPadOS, the app requests Google's UMP consent information. If Google reports that ads cannot yet be requested and a consent form is available, the app presents that form and then rechecks the result. Interstitial requests are blocked when the consent state does not permit ad requests. The in-app privacy control can open Google's privacy-options form when the SDK provides it, after which the app rechecks consent before preparing ads.

Users can also review Apple's device privacy and tracking controls and Google's advertising controls at Google Ads Settings. Available choices and ad behavior vary by location, law, device, consent choice, and the applicable service settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Local language, progress, score, star, and ad-frequency records remain on the device until the user deletes the app or uses an available iOS storage/reset action. The developer does not operate an account system for this game. Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and controls; the developer does not control Google's retention periods. Privacy questions about developer-controlled information can be sent to the contact address below.

7. Children's privacy

The game does not require an account and does not knowingly ask children or adults to submit personal information directly to the developer. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The App Store age-rating, child-directed treatment, advertising, and App Privacy settings are maintained consistently with the current product and applicable law. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us so that we can review the request.
