Bunny Apples (小兔数苹果) — Privacy Policy

Platform: Android / Google Play
Package: com.xuzengfei.bunnyapples
Developer: Everyday Game Studio
Effective / last updated: October 28, 2026

Data collection

The developer does not operate an account system, display ads or use its own gameplay analytics in the app. Gameplay works offline. The app connects to Google Play to check available products, purchases and restored access.

Purchases and Google Play

A parent or guardian may choose to make a one-time purchase to unlock additional content. Google Play processes the transaction and may collect information under Google’s Privacy Policy. The app receives purchase status and stores an access record on the device; it does not receive your payment card details. A network connection is needed to purchase or restore access.

Billing diagnostics

The Google Play Billing library can send technical diagnostic events to Google when it connects to Play and processes billing operations, including response codes and limited error details. The transport component may attach technical information such as app version, device model, manufacturer, Android version and build, language, region, network type, mobile network code and time zone. Google receives and processes this information under its Privacy Policy for the operation, reliability and improvement of its billing services. The developer does not receive these diagnostic logs. These connections may occur when the app checks purchase availability or ownership, including without a completed purchase.

Local progress

Fruit totals, progress and sound preferences are stored only on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may remove them, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them only to respond to your request. Avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. You may ask us to delete support correspondence.

Children

The app is designed for early mathematics practice and does not ask children to submit personal information. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions.
