About the app

Bunny Apples (小兔数苹果) is an early-math learning game for young children on iPhone and iPad. Children count apples, compare quantities, and practice addition and subtraction within 20 with a friendly bunny. This policy applies to application com.xuzengfei.bunnyapples, provided by 余芳 伍.

We do not collect any data

The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. There is no account, sign-in, chat, social feature, advertising, in-app purchase, analytics, crash reporting, or cloud service. The app does not connect to the internet and contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data.

Information on your device

Questions are generated on the device during play. The app does not keep a player profile or send gameplay results anywhere. The sound on/off switch applies only while the app is open.

Spoken questions

The app reads questions aloud using the text-to-speech voice built into iOS and iPadOS. The spoken text is fixed game text such as numbers and questions; it never includes information about your child. Speech is produced by Apple's system service under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app does not use the microphone.

Permissions

The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, tracking (App Tracking Transparency), notifications, or any other protected resource.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and complies with the requirements for apps in the App Store Kids category. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, there is nothing for parents to review or delete. The app contains no external links, advertising, or purchase options that children could reach.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and its date before the new version of the app is released.
