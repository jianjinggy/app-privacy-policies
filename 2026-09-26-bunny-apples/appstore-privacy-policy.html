Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, share or sell personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, account system, in-app purchases or external links, and gameplay does not require an Internet connection.

Local progress

Fruit totals, progress and sound preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting the app or its local data may remove them, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them only to respond to your request. Avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. You may ask us to delete support correspondence.

Children

The app is designed for early mathematics practice and does not ask children to submit personal information. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions.
