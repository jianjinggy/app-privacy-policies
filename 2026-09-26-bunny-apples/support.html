How to play

Choose a picture card on the home screen: counting, comparing, or addition and subtraction within 5, 10 or 20. Each round has five questions. Tap the answer button with the right number or symbol. In counting mode, tap the apples to number them one by one. Tap the yellow speaker to hear the question again, and the green speaker to turn sound on or off.
