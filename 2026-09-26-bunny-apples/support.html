Bunny Apples (小兔数苹果) — Support

Offline early-math play for iPhone, iPad and Android.

How to play

Choose counting, comparing, addition, subtraction, mixed practice or the matching-card game. Arithmetic modes offer number ranges; the matching game lets children choose how many cards to use. Questions and numbers can be read aloud by the device.

Progress

Fruit totals and preferences stay on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing local storage may remove them. There is no account or cloud-save service.
