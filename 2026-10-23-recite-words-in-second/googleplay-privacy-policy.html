Offline learning and local storage

The app includes its vocabulary content, illustrations, English pronunciation recordings and 9,510 word meanings. The learning features do not require an internet connection or an online account.

Scores, chapter progress, language selection and volume preferences are stored on your device. The developer does not receive this learning data, and there is no developer-operated gameplay server or cloud-sync service.

Data collection, advertising and permissions

The app itself does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer or third parties. It makes no application network requests, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking. There are no in-app purchases.

The app does not declare the Android INTERNET permission and does not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, location or contacts. English pronunciation uses recordings bundled with the app; your voice is not recorded.

External services you choose to use

The rating entry opens the relevant app store outside the app. The store and operating system handle that interaction under their own privacy practices. The developer does not receive your local scores or progress through this entry.

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use that information to respond to your request. Your email service provider and our email service provider process the correspondence as part of delivering and storing email. Please send only information needed to explain the problem.

Retention, deletion and your choices

There is no app account to delete. You can remove local learning data by uninstalling the app or using your operating system’s app-data removal controls, where available. Uninstalling or clearing storage may permanently remove your progress.

Operating-system or device backups may retain a copy of app data and may restore it later. Manage or delete those copies through the relevant system backup settings; the developer cannot access or erase them for you.

Children and device security

The offline learning features do not ask users, including children, to provide personal information. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. A parent or guardian can contact us about support correspondence concerning a child.

Local data are protected by your device’s operating-system controls. Keep your device secure and review your system backup settings.
