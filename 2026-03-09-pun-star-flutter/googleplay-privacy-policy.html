Game and local storage

This picture-and-word puzzle game does not require an account. It has no cloud save, no in-app purchases and no developer-operated account or gameplay server. Level progress, the selected Simplified or Traditional Chinese content version, and sound preferences are stored on your device. The two content versions have separate progress. Local operating-system backup may preserve these settings. Removing the app and its app data removes local game data, subject to your device backup settings.

Advertising and SDK data

The game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). It includes interstitial advertisements between eligible levels and optional rewarded advertisements for answer hints. Ads require a network connection. The game's code does not turn off ad personalization: whether ads are personalized depends on your UMP consent choices and your device settings.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device and advertising identifiers, interactions such as launches, taps and video views, advertising impression information, crash logs, performance metrics and other diagnostic data. These support advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and SDK reliability. The developer does not ask you to provide precise GPS location, contacts, health data, payment details or account credentials to play.

Privacy choices

UMP updates privacy information when the app starts and presents a privacy message when required. When available for your region and consent status, the in-game settings provide a Privacy options entry point to review your choices. A consent decision is handled by the SDK and does not affect access to the normal puzzle game. Rewarded hints are optional and are available only after an advertisement awards the reward.

On Android, the game keeps the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and keeps Google's publisher first-party ID enabled. Google's advertising services may use the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and other permitted identifiers to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads. The game's code does not turn off ad personalization: whether ads are personalized depends on your UMP consent choices and your device settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices Settings → Privacy → Ads); the exact path depends on your Android version. Resetting or deleting the advertising ID does not stop all SDK data processing.

Sharing, security and retention

Advertising-related data is processed by Google and its advertising services, rather than being sent to a developer gameplay server. Google determines SDK data retention and applicable deletion controls. Advertising network requests use transport encryption. Local progress remains on your device until app data is removed. We do not sell a developer-maintained user database.

The game has no developer account, so there is no account to delete. To delete local game data, uninstall the app or clear its app data, subject to operating-system backups. To request deletion of data we hold, such as support correspondence, email q908267264@gmail.com with the app name and platform.

Children and support

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older, and is not directed to children under 13. If you contact the developer by email, the address and contents of your message are used to answer your request.
