Common questions

Rewarded ads: Ads depend on network connectivity and availability. A reward is granted after the ad has completed. If an ad is unavailable, please try again later.

Game progress: Progress and settings are stored on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data may remove that progress.

Sound and language: Open the in-game settings to adjust music and effects. The game supports 21 languages.
