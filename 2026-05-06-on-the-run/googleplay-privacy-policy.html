1. App and developer

This policy applies to Full Throttle Rush (一路狂飙), the five-lane racing game provided for Android · Google Play by Everyday Game Studio. Its application identifier is com.xuzengfei.fullthrottlerush. This page covers this platform only; the other platform has its own policy.

2. Game records and purchases

Vehicle unlocks and upgrades, coins, boosters, missions, personal records, tutorial progress, reward recovery records, language and audio preferences are stored on your device. The game has no player account, developer-operated cloud save, chat or online leaderboard. The game code does not send your saved racing progress to a developer server. Vehicle and booster purchases use in-game currency; the current game does not offer real-money in-app purchases or subscriptions and does not ask for payment-card details.

3. Advertising and privacy messages

The game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). It requests non-personalized rewarded ads. Watching an ad is voluntary and can earn coins, boosters or a revival when completed. Ads may be loaded before you choose to watch one, so SDK network processing is not limited to the moment you press the watch button. The game does not integrate a separate analytics, crash-reporting or advertising mediation SDK.

Advertising service data

Google’s SDK collects and shares IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostic/performance information, and device or account identifiers available to the SDK. Purposes include advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states that its SDK encrypts transmitted data using TLS. UMP processes consent status and choices. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing; they are not a promise of zero collection.

4. Identifiers and device access

The Android advertising SDK can process the advertising ID, app set ID and other identifiers available under system and SDK settings. The game does not remove the SDK advertising-ID permission. You can use Android advertising settings to reset or delete your advertising ID; available controls vary by Android version. The game does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise GPS location. Network access is used for advertising services and external pages.

5. Your choices

Settings → Privacy policy opens this page. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings also shows Advertising privacy options to review or change applicable choices. Device privacy settings provide additional controls. You can decline voluntary ad viewing; playing and earning through gameplay do not require watching a rewarded ad. Opening an ad, rating link or external site may take you to a third party governed by its own policy.

6. Retention, deletion and support

Local progress remains until removed by operating-system app-data controls or uninstalling, subject to device backup and restore settings. Removing local data does not necessarily erase information already held by advertising providers. Google determines retention for the data it controls under its policies. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments and use them to respond, resolve the request and retain necessary support records. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.

7. Rights, international processing and security

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a supervisory authority. Contact us for developer-controlled information; Google-controlled information may require a request to Google. Providers may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards. We use reasonable measures for information we handle, but no device storage or network transmission is guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children and updates

The game does not ask players to enter personal information to race. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address the concern. If the game’s data practices or contact details change, we will update this page and the date shown above.

9. This website

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Viewing it sends ordinary web-request information, including IP address, to GitHub. The page itself adds no analytics scripts, ads or cookies. Third-party policies are linked below.

1. 应用与开发者

本政策适用于由 Everyday Game Studio 在 Android · Google Play 提供的五车道赛车游戏《一路狂飙》（Full Throttle Rush），应用标识为 com.xuzengfei.fullthrottlerush。本页仅对应上述平台，另一平台另有独立政策。

2. 游戏记录与购买

车辆解锁与升级、金币、道具、任务、个人纪录、教程进度、奖励恢复记录、语言和音量偏好保存在您的设备上。游戏没有玩家账号、开发者运营的云存档、聊天或在线排行榜，游戏代码不会将您的赛车存档上传至开发者服务器。车辆和道具使用游戏内货币购买；当前游戏没有现金内购或订阅，也不要求提供银行卡资料。

3. 广告与隐私消息

游戏集成 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）和 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP），请求非个性化奖励广告。您可以自愿观看广告，完成后获得金币、道具或复活奖励。广告可能在您选择观看前预加载，因此 SDK 的网络数据处理不只发生在点击观看按钮时。游戏没有集成独立的统计分析、崩溃上报或广告聚合 SDK。

广告服务数据

Google SDK 会收集和共享 IP 地址（可据此估计大致位置）、应用启动、点击和视频观看等互动、诊断及性能信息，以及 SDK 可获取的设备或账号标识符，用于广告、分析和反欺诈。Google 说明其 SDK 使用 TLS 加密传输数据。 UMP 处理同意状态及选择。非个性化广告仍会涉及数据处理，并不代表完全不收集数据。

4. 标识符与设备权限

Android 广告 SDK 可以处理广告 ID、应用集 ID，以及系统和 SDK 设置允许的其他标识符。游戏未移除 SDK 的广告 ID 权限。您可以使用 Android 广告设置重置或删除广告 ID；具体选项因系统版本而异。 游戏不请求通讯录、相机、麦克风、照片或精确 GPS 定位权限。网络访问用于广告服务和外部页面。

5. 您的选择

在“设置 → 隐私政策”中可随时打开本页。Google 要求提供隐私选项入口时，设置中还会显示广告隐私选项，供您查看或修改适用的选择。设备隐私设置提供其他控制。您可以不观看自愿奖励广告；正常游玩和通过比赛获得收益不要求观看奖励广告。点击广告、评分链接或外部网站可能跳转到适用其自身政策的第三方服务。

6. 保存、删除与客服

本地记录会保留至您通过系统应用数据管理或卸载移除，具体还受设备备份和恢复设置影响。删除本地数据不一定删除广告服务商已持有的信息。Google 根据其政策决定其控制的数据的保存期限。您通过邮件联系我们时，我们会收到您的邮箱地址、邮件内容和附件，用于回复、处理请求及保留必要的客服记录。请勿发送密码或不必要的个人信息。

7. 权利、跨境处理与安全

根据您所在地的适用规则，您可能享有访问、更正、删除个人数据，反对或限制处理、撤回同意以及向监管机构投诉的权利。涉及开发者控制的信息请联系我们；涉及 Google 控制的信息可能需要向 Google 提出请求。服务商可能在您所在国家或地区以外处理信息，并适用其相应保障措施。我们会对经手的信息采取合理保护，但无法保证设备存储和网络传输绝对安全。

8. 儿童与政策更新

游戏不要求玩家输入个人资料才能开始比赛。如果家长或监护人认为儿童已向我们提供个人信息，请联系我们核实和处理。游戏数据处理方式或联系方式变化时，我们会更新本页及日期。
