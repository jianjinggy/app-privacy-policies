Platform: Android / Google Play
Effective date: October 12, 2026

Google Play publisher: Everyday Game Studio. Android advertising IDs can be reset or deleted in your device settings.

Local game data

The game stores progress, preferences, tutorial completion and review-prompt history on your device. We do not operate a player-account system, cloud save service, or our own analytics service. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, subject to operating-system backup settings.

Advertising and consent

The mobile game integrates Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform. Advertising services may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, app interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve service performance. Data may be shared with Google and advertising partners according to consent and applicable service policies. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing.

Rewarded ads are optional. Declining an ad does not prevent normal play. Where available, open the privacy options in the game's settings to review or change consent. Mobile advertising identifiers can also be controlled in your device settings.

Device functions

The game checks connection availability and audio-interruption state locally to manage advertising and game sound. These checks do not read call content or record audio. The game does not request camera, microphone or precise-location access. Store-rating actions open the platform's rating service.

Security and retention

Google describes its advertising data transmission as encrypted using TLS. Third-party retention is governed by the providers' policies; we do not set or guarantee their retention periods. We cannot directly access or delete advertising identifiers or third-party records. Use device privacy controls and Google's privacy tools for those records.

Audience

This game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and above and is not designed specifically for children under 13.
