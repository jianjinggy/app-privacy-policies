1. Scope

Echo Court is a basketball physics puzzle game. This policy explains the game’s local records, advertising services and privacy choices. No developer account, social login, chat, payment or in-app purchase is offered in this version.

2. Records on your device

Level progress, stars, language, music and effect volumes, motion preferences, the version for which a review was requested, and the most recent rewarded-ad presentation time are saved locally. Gameplay records are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. They may be included in operating-system backups. Local records remain until app data are removed, subject to backup and restore settings.

3. Google advertising

The game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). It offers optional rewarded ads for a shot guide and may show interstitial ads at breaks after a level. A requested ad can be unavailable; normal shooting and level retries remain available.

Google and its advertising service providers may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, viewed ads, crash and diagnostic information, and performance data. These support ad delivery and measurement, analytics, service operation and fraud prevention. Ads without personalization can still involve data processing. Google advertising traffic is encrypted in transit.

The game does not separately integrate a developer analytics account or send your level-by-level records to a custom backend. Google’s own services determine their data retention and processing under their policies.

4. Identifiers and privacy choices

Google’s advertising services may use the Android advertising ID and app set ID, subject to system settings and applicable choices. You can reset or delete the advertising ID using Android’s advertising controls. The game does not request phone-state, shared-storage, camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions.

UMP consent information is refreshed at launch. Ads are requested only when Google’s actual consent API permits requests. Where required, the game shows Google’s consent form and makes Privacy choices available in Settings. You can revisit that entry to change your choices. Settings also links to this policy. Ad personalization depends on applicable consent and device settings; the game does not override your privacy choice.

5. Deletion, rights and international processing

There is no developer account to delete. To remove local game records, use the operating system’s app-data controls or uninstall the app, taking backups into account. If you contact support, we receive the details you choose to send and use them to handle your request. To request access to or deletion of support correspondence or other developer-controlled information, email q908267264@gmail.com with the game name and platform.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or contact a privacy authority. Requests concerning Google-controlled data may also need to be directed to Google. Google and hosting services may process data outside your country under their applicable safeguards. No storage or network service can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children and policy changes

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not designed specifically for children under 13. It does not ask players to submit personal information to play. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided information to us can contact support. We will review the concern and take appropriate action. Material changes to these practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
