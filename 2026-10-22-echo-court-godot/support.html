回声球场 / 回聲球場

How to play

Drag backwards to set your angle and power, then release to shoot. Score in every hoop within the available shots. During flight, use Echo to send a twin ball in the other direction. The level map lets you revisit unlocked stages.

Sound and language

Open Pause → Settings to change language, music and sound-effect volume. Progress stays on your device. Uninstalling can remove it; device backups may preserve it.

Ads and privacy

The shot guide is optional. Advertising availability depends on the network and privacy choices. Normal shooting and retries remain available. When required, Settings → Privacy choices opens Google’s privacy options.
