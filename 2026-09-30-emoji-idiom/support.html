About the game

Emoji Idiom is a Chinese four-character idiom puzzle game. Interpret the illustrated clues and select characters from the board. It supports Simplified and Traditional Chinese. The first level highlights the real answer buttons step by step whenever you revisit it.

Settings and saved progress

Use the gear icon to open Settings. Music and sound effects have separate volume sliders. You can switch the Chinese script or reset progress. Resetting progress is confirmed twice and preserves preferences. Progress is stored locally; there is no player login or game-operated cloud synchronization. Removing app data or restoring a device backup can affect progress.

Hints and advertising

A rewarded hint requires your confirmation. Cancelling the offer does not grant a reward or request an ad. A reward is applied after the advertising SDK reports it earned and the advertisement closes. Closing early without a reward does not grant a hint. Offline devices cannot use the ad-based hint path. When ads are unavailable, the game reports the condition instead of requiring you to approve tracking.

Privacy options appear in Settings when Google UMP requires them. On iOS, tracking permission can also be changed in system Settings. Ad availability is controlled partly by the advertising service and consent status.

Audio

Set either audio slider to zero to mute it. Background music pauses during full-screen ads and when the game leaves the foreground. If other audio is detected at startup, the game keeps its soundtrack off for that launch; sound effects remain independently controlled. The game does not record audio.
