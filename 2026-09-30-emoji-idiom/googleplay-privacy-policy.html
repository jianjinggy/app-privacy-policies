Scope

This policy covers the Android version of Emoji Idiom, the Chinese idiom puzzle game published by Everyday Game Studio.

Game data stored on your device

The game saves level progress, stars, in-game coins, favorites, check-ins, daily challenges, an unfinished answer, language and audio/haptic preferences on your device. It also stores a requested-review version and recent rewarded-ad display times to enforce game rules. We do not operate player accounts, a game-specific cloud-save service, a leaderboard server or in-app purchases. The game does not ask for your name, contacts, photos, precise location or microphone recordings.

The device may maintain operating-system backups according to your settings. Such backups are separate from a cloud service operated by the game. The game checks network availability locally and checks whether other audio is playing to manage its soundtrack; it does not record that audio or upload these checks to a game-specific server.

Advertising and consent

The game integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Rewarded advertisements are offered voluntarily for hints, and interstitial advertisements may appear at level-end pauses. UMP updates consent information and shows a consent form when required. Advertising requests are permitted only when UMP reports that ads may be requested.

Google's advertising services may process device identifiers, an IP address and the approximate location inferred from it, advertising impressions and interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. These are used to provide and measure advertising, analyze performance, and protect against invalid activity. This processing is separate from the game's locally saved progress. Google and its advertising partners process information under their own policies; see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's ad controls.

Android identifiers and permissions

The advertising SDK can use the Android advertising ID, app set ID and other permitted device/account-bound identifiers. Advertising ID and applicable AdServices permissions are retained for SDK operation. Android settings provide advertising-ID reset or deletion controls, depending on your system version. Network-state access is used for the local connection check. The game does not use device permissions to read your contacts, photos or audio recordings.

Google's Android SDK disclosure describes TLS encryption for user data sent by its advertising SDK. Local game saves are device-local records, not a claim of encrypted cloud storage.

Your choices

Settings includes a Privacy options entry when UMP requires one. You can use it to revisit the available consent choices. You can decline an offered rewarded advertisement and continue playing. Available identifiers and personalization depend on your choices, the platform and the advertising SDK; a permission refusal is not treated as permission to track.

Resetting and deleting data

Reset progress in Settings resets gameplay progress and in-game balances, favorites and check-ins while retaining preferences. It does not erase Google's records or advertising consent preferences. Removing the app or clearing its app data removes device-local app records; operating-system backup copies are controlled by your backup settings. Advertising-provider records have their own retention and deletion controls. Contact us for questions; we do not promise that resetting the game deletes information already processed by an advertising provider.

Support information

If you email us, we use the email and information you choose to provide to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, payment information or unrelated personal information. We do not automatically send gameplay saves to support.

Children and age suitability

The game has no child accounts and does not ask players to submit their age. Parents and guardians should review the applicable store age rating and device parental controls and supervise younger players. If you believe a child supplied personal information through a support request, please contact us so we can address it. Store ratings and advertising/privacy choices are separate matters.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions or help with local data deletion, email q908267264@gmail.com.
