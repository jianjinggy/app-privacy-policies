Flower Link Puzzle Privacy Policy

iOS and iPadOS · zhufeng Xu
Updated November 26, 2026. Applies to version 2.0.0 and later.

What the game stores

Your stars, unlocked levels, coins, items, daily rewards, language, and sound and vibration preferences are saved on your device. The game has no sign-in or developer-operated gameplay server. It has no in-app purchases and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

Advertising and consent

We use Google AdMob for interstitial and optional rewarded ads. As described in its SDK documentation, Google may collect and share information such as IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device identifiers, ad views and other interactions, and crash and performance information. These are used to provide and measure advertising, diagnose and improve the service, and detect abuse. The information available depends on your settings, region, and choices.

Google’s User Messaging Platform presents choices where required. When available, open the game’s Settings and select Ad privacy options to change your choices. You can change tracking permission in Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. The game remains playable if you decline.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps.

Support, retention and deletion

If you email us, we use your address and message to respond. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve the issue and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to access or delete information we hold by contacting the address below. Google controls retention and choices for data it handles. To delete the local game save, clear the app data where supported or uninstall the game. Any operating-system backup is managed by your device settings.

Children and privacy rights

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us. Depending on your region, you may be able to access, correct, delete or restrict your data or object to processing.
