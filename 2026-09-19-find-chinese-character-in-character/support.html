字里找字 · Game Support

Chinese character stroke puzzles for iPhone, iPad and Android.

How to play

Start from the main menu, choose a level, then select strokes from the large character to form smaller characters. Submit your selection and find all required answers to complete the level. Settings lets you switch Simplified or Traditional Chinese and sound. Progress is stored locally and separately for each language.

Progress and ads

Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove progress. Rewarded hints depend on ad availability; if no ad is available, you can continue solving the puzzle. Applicable advertising privacy choices are available through 隐私设置 in Settings when required by Google.
