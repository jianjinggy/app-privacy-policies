About this policy

This policy covers 字里找字 (字裡找字), the Chinese character stroke puzzle game provided by 余芳 伍 for iOS / iPadOS · App Store. The other platform has a separate policy.

Game data and local storage

Language preferences, sound settings, unlocked levels and puzzle progress are stored on your device. The game has no player account, cloud save, chat, online leaderboard, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Puzzle content is included in the app. We do not operate a server that receives your saved puzzle progress.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads that provide puzzle hints. Ads may be loaded before they are displayed. It also uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain applicable consent information and display privacy messages where required. Advertising availability and personalization depend on your choices, platform permissions and Google's applicable controls. Non-personalized or limited ads can still involve data processing.

Information processed by Google services

Google's advertising and consent services may collect and share IP addresses and inferred approximate location; device and app information and permitted identifiers; app launches, taps, video views and other app/ad interactions; advertisements viewed; and crash, performance and other diagnostic information. These support advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and operation of advertising and consent services. Data can be associated with a device or app identifier even without a player account. We do not integrate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service or additional mediation adapters.

Identifiers and permissions

After any required Google UMP consent message, the app requests Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization before requesting ads. If you decline ATT or tracking is restricted, the app uses restricted advertising settings and does not enable its publisher first-party identifier. The IDFA is available to advertising services only when iOS authorizes tracking. You can change the system tracking choice in iOS Settings. Google may still process permitted app- or developer-scoped information for limited advertising and measurement.

The game does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise GPS location. Google estimates general location from network information rather than GPS permission. Google's SDK traffic is encrypted in transit.

Your privacy choices

When required, Google displays a privacy consent form. When an advertising privacy-options entry is required, the in-game Settings menu shows 隐私设置 (Privacy settings) so you can revisit available choices. Gameplay remains available when ads are unavailable. Rewarded hint viewing is optional. Device privacy controls provide additional choices. External sites or advertisers opened from an ad are governed by their own policies.

Retention, deletion and support

Local progress remains until you remove app data or uninstall, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior. We cannot identify or delete Google's device-level advertising records through a game account because there is no such account. Google determines retention and available deletion controls for data it handles. Uninstalling does not necessarily delete information previously received by Google. Contact us about developer-controlled support records. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments and use them to respond and keep necessary support records; avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Children, rights and international processing

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a regulator. Providers may process information outside your country according to their applicable safeguards. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.
