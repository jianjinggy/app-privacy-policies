Word Hexa Link Privacy Policy

App identifier: com.xuforge.wordhive
Effective and last updated: October 21, 2026

About this game

Word Hexa Link is a single-player word puzzle provided by Yufang Wu. This policy covers the iOS / iPadOS version, identified as com.xuforge.wordhive. You do not need an account. The game has no cloud save service, chat, user uploads, or in-app purchases.

Progress stored on your device

The game stores level stars, best scores, words found and other play statistics, daily results, sound and language preferences, the version for which an automatic review was requested, and the last actual rewarded-ad display time on your device. Our game code does not send your puzzle answers or progress to a developer server. We do not add a separate analytics SDK. Operating-system and store services may process information under their own policies.

Advertising and privacy messages

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. Rewarded video hints are optional. Interstitial ads can appear between completed levels when the game’s timing and privacy conditions allow. Ads are requested only when the native UMP service permits requests. UMP may process consent choices and related information needed to manage and remember them.

Data handled by Google

Advertising services may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, app and device information, identifiers permitted by your settings, ad requests and views, taps and video interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics. These are used for advertising, measurement, service operation, analytics, and detecting fraud or abuse. Google receives and may share this information with advertising partners under its policies. On iOS, identifiers may include app- or developer-scoped identifiers and, with the required tracking authorization, the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Your privacy choices

The game requests Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission before continuing its UMP flow. If you decline tracking or iOS does not provide that permission, the game continues through UMP without treating your choice as consent. IDFA access depends on iOS authorization. You can change tracking permission in the device’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where UMP requires it, Settings in the game includes Privacy options, which opens Google’s privacy-choice form. Ad personalization depends on the applicable consent choices, regional requirements and device settings; the ability to request ads alone does not mean you agreed to personalized advertising. Declining tracking or applicable ad-personalization consent does not block puzzle play.

Permissions and store features

The game does not request camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise-location access. Tracking authorization is used for the advertising purpose described above. The optional rating action opens the App Store; the game can also request Apple’s system review interface. Apple decides whether to show it.

Retention, deletion and requests

You can reset puzzle progress from the game’s Settings. Uninstalling the app or using operating-system app-data controls removes local records, subject to the operating system’s backup and restore behavior. There is no developer account to delete. Google determines retention for information it controls. If you contact us for support, we use the details you provide to respond and handle your request. To request access, correction or deletion of information we control, or exercise applicable privacy rights, contact q1492307427@gmail.com with the app name, platform and nature of your request. Requests about Google-controlled data may also need to be directed to Google. Services may process data outside your country under their applicable safeguards.

Children and changes

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information to us. A parent or guardian with a concern may contact us. We use reasonable safeguards, but no device storage or network service is completely secure. If the game’s practices change, we will update this policy and its date.
