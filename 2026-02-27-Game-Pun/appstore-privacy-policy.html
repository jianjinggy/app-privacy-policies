1. About this game

This policy applies to 谐音梗猜猜猜 - 看图解谜游戏 (com.zengfei.pun), provided by zhufeng Xu for iOS / iPadOS · App Store. The game offers picture-based Chinese word puzzles, keyboard and candidate-character modes, and selectable Simplified and Traditional Chinese question lists. The developer does not operate an in-game registration system, chat service or payment system.

2. Progress and settings

Completed question IDs, game progress, language and sound preferences, review-prompt status and pending leaderboard scores are stored on your device. These local puzzle records are not sent to a developer-operated server. Device backup and restore services may retain app data according to your system settings.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP addresses (used to infer approximate location), device identifiers, app and advertising interactions such as launches, taps and video views, and crash or performance diagnostics. Google processes these data to deliver and measure advertising, analyze service performance, and prevent fraud. On iOS, this app does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization or use IDFA for personalized targeting. Other identifiers, including the identifier for vendor where available, may still be processed by the SDK.

4. Privacy choices

Google’s User Messaging Platform may display privacy messages according to your region and applicable settings. Where required by the SDK, the game settings provide an advertising privacy options entry so that you can review or change your choices. Non-personalized advertising may still involve storage, identifiers and measurement. The game does not request precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo-library access.

5. Platform leaderboards

Your leaderboard profile and score may be visible to other players according to your platform privacy settings. You can manage this feature through your platform account settings. Playing puzzles does not require creating an account with the developer.
