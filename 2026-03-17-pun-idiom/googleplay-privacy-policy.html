谐音梗猜成语 — Privacy Policy

Platform: Android
Application: com.zengfei.pun.idiom
Effective and last updated: September 13, 2026

This policy applies to 谐音梗猜成语 (諧音梗猜成語), a picture and homophone idiom puzzle game. Read the other platform policy.

Local gameplay information

Puzzle progress, language selection and sound preferences are stored on your device. The game does not upload these gameplay records to a developer-operated server. Removing the app or clearing its storage removes local records, subject to operating-system backups. The game has no user accounts, chat, in-app purchases or user-generated content.

Advertising and information collected by Google

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads that reveal a puzzle explanation. Advertising services send data directly to Google even though the developer does not operate a gameplay server.

Google's SDK may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP addresses, device and app identifiers, app/device information, ad requests, impressions, taps, video interactions, performance information and other diagnostics. This processing supports advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security and service reliability. Identifier availability depends on platform permissions, device settings and SDK configuration. The app has no separate Firebase, Unity Ads or standalone analytics integration.

On Android, the SDK may process the Android advertising ID, app set ID and other device or app identifiers subject to Google and Android settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in supported Android versions.

We do not request precise-location, contact, camera, photo-library or microphone access for puzzle gameplay. Approximate location can still be inferred by advertising services from network information without precise-location permission.

Choices, retention and security

You may decline optional rewarded ads and continue solving puzzles. Manage advertising privacy through your device's privacy settings and Google's advertising controls. These controls do not necessarily prevent every form of SDK data processing.

Google documents encryption in transit for Mobile Ads SDK data. Google and its service providers manage SDK data retention according to their policies and applicable law; the developer cannot promise immediate deletion of data controlled independently by Google. Services and hosting providers may process data outside your country. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.
