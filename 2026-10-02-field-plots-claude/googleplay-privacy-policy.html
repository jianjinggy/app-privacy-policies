About this policy

This policy applies to Field Plots (分田小农夫), a farm logic puzzle game provided by Everyday Game Studio for Android. The iOS and iPadOS edition has a separate policy.

Information stored on your device

The game stores puzzle progress, settings, completion records, best times, daily-field state, collected crops, unlocked farm buildings, pending discoveries, and the timestamp of the last rewarded-ad display locally in the app sandbox. This information is used to operate the game, restore progress, prevent duplicate rewards, and apply advertising frequency limits. The game does not require an account and has no cloud save, chat, remote leaderboard, or developer analytics server.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes local game records, subject to operating-system backup behavior.

Advertising and Google services

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to provide optional rewarded hint ads and post-level interstitial ads, and uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage applicable advertising privacy choices. Depending on your region, device settings, and privacy choices, Google may collect or process device or other identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID), approximate location derived from network information, app interactions, advertising data, and diagnostics/performance information for advertising, fraud prevention, security, measurement, and service operation. Google acts as an independent controller or service provider for information processed by its SDK.

Resetting or deleting the Android Advertising ID in system settings limits personalized advertising under Google's rules, though SDKs may still use other signals as permitted by applicable consent. Ad requests occur only when UMP permits ad requests.

When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point in your region, it is available in Settings → Privacy options. Advertising unavailability or declining optional choices never blocks normal puzzle play. Optional rewarded hints ask for confirmation before showing an ad and grant the hint after the SDK confirms reward completion and the ad closes. Post-level interstitial ads follow frequency pacing and never appear during tutorial levels.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Permissions and other services

The game does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location. Network connectivity checks inspect local device network interfaces before requesting an ad. Network permissions and Advertising ID / AdServices permissions are used by Google AdMob and UMP to deliver ads and manage privacy consent, transmitting standard network metadata such as your IP address to Google over encrypted connections. The current version has no in-app purchases and includes no separate third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Retention, security, and international processing

Local game records remain on your device until you clear progress in Settings, clear app data, or uninstall the app. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and message contents solely to reply and keep necessary support records. Google governs retention and deletion controls for data handled by its services and may process data outside your country under applicable safeguards.

Children and your choices

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, withdraw consent, or object to processing. You can revisit available advertising choices in-app and manage Advertising ID preferences in Android Settings.

Changes

We will update this policy and its date whenever our data practices, third-party services, or contact details materially change.
