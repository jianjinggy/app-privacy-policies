Match Move - Matchstick Puzzle
Privacy Policy

iOS · Updated and effective November 2, 2026

This policy describes how zhufeng Xu and the service providers used by Match Move - Matchstick Puzzle handle information. The app can be played without creating an account.

Game data on your device

Progress, preferences and game or training results are stored on your device. Removing the app may remove this local data. Operating-system backups may retain a copy under your device settings. We do not operate an account service for this app.

Advertising and service data

The app uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform, and Unity Ads through Unity LevelPlay. Advertising providers may process app or device identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, device and network information, ad impressions and interactions, app activity, and crash or performance diagnostics to deliver ads, measure their effectiveness, prevent fraud and improve their services. Unity also discloses processing of purchase history or purchase tendencies and other usage information. These disclosures do not mean that this app asks for payment-card or banking information.

The Unity engine also includes Unity Analytics and performance services; they may process app events, session information, identifiers and diagnostics to maintain and improve the game.

Providers receive and may share information with their advertising, measurement and service partners as described in their policies. Network transmission by these advertising SDKs is encrypted. Some service information is associated with app or device identifiers; it is not necessarily anonymous. Processing may take place outside your country.

Your choices

Where required, the app presents a Google consent message before requesting ads. When available, use the app's advertising privacy option to review or change your choices. The choices made in that message are passed to supported advertising providers; choosing non-personalized ads does not mean that all service data collection stops.

On iOS, the system may ask whether you allow tracking. You can decline and can change this choice later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining does not require you to stop playing. Tracking that requires this permission is not authorized by merely accepting a consent message.

Optional gameplay analytics

Starting with version 26.11.2, build 26110202, the app offers “Share gameplay data” in Settings. Earlier builds do not enable this feature. The feature uses Google Analytics for Firebase, is off by default, and starts collecting only if you choose Allow in its explanation dialog. You can play normally without allowing it.

If you allow this feature, it may collect an app-instance analytics identifier, app version and interface language, device and operating-system information, sessions and foreground/background activity, puzzle attempts and results, active play time, hint requests and delivery, advertising-flow events, and impression-level revenue estimates supplied by the ad SDKs, including currency and precision. Google may process IP addresses for approximate location, service operation and spam detection under its region-specific Analytics practices. We use these data to understand puzzle difficulty, hint reliability, advertising flows and game quality.

For this optional feature, we disable collection of advertising identifiers and the identifier for vendor, do not set a personal user ID, and do not request GPS, contacts, photos, names, email addresses or an account login. An app-instance identifier can associate events within the app and is a pseudonymous identifier, so this feature is not described as fully anonymous. These settings apply to optional gameplay analytics; the advertising services and their separate ATT and consent choices are described above.

Turning this choice off stops future collection by this feature and resets its local analytics data and identifier. It does not automatically delete information already sent to Google. User-level and event-level retention is set to two months, with the retention period not reset by new activity; standard aggregate reports may be retained longer. For access or deletion requests concerning earlier data, use the contact details below. Processing may take place outside your country.
