Game help

Tap suspicious details to find clues, then choose the person who explains the evidence. Small details matter. The first case's tutorial is available whenever you replay it. Music and sound effects have separate controls in Settings.

Progress and reset

Progress is saved locally. Settings > Reset Progress requires a second confirmation and clears the local game save. There is no account or cloud recovery service in this game. Device backups are controlled by your operating system.

Ads and clues

The clue button first offers Cancel or Watch Ad outside tutorials. An early-closed ad without a reward does not grant an ad reward. If ads are unavailable, the game may offer a clue after its waiting period. When required by UMP, Settings shows Privacy Options. An ad's availability depends on network, consent and the advertising service.
