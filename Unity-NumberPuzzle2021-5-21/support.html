Number Puzzle Support

Slide numbers into order, one tile at a time.

How to play

Tap a tile beside the empty space to slide it. Arrange the numbers from left to right, row by row, leaving the empty space at the bottom right.

Three ways to play

Challenges offer 30 fixed 3×3 levels. Daily Puzzle gives you one board each day. Classic lets you choose 2×2 to 10×10 boards and aim for your best time.

Themes and progress

Complete five different challenge levels to unlock Cream Ceramic. Switch themes and languages in Settings. Progress and personal scores are stored on your device.

Updating from the original game

Install the update over the existing app to retain its local scores. Uninstalling first or clearing app storage removes the previous local data and may prevent migration.
