Game progress

The game does not use an account. Puzzle progress, collected idioms, and settings are stored on your device. You can play the puzzles offline. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data removes that local information from the device, subject to any operating-system backups.

Advertising

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). The crossword can show a banner, eligible stage clears can show an interstitial ad, and optional hints can use rewarded ads. Ads need a network connection.

Google’s SDKs may collect or share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, ads viewed, and crash, performance and diagnostic data. Google can use these data to serve and measure ads, provide analytics, prevent fraud, and operate and improve its services. Personalization depends on the permissions and consent available for your device and region. We do not upload your puzzle answers or progress to a developer server.

Privacy choices

On Android, Google’s advertising services may use the Android advertising ID and other identifiers permitted by your device settings. You can review, reset or delete the advertising ID in your device’s ad privacy settings. The location of those settings depends on the Android version.

Google’s privacy messages appear when required. When available, open Settings in the game and choose 広告のプライバシー設定 to review the choices. You can read the Google Privacy Policy for details about Google’s handling of data.
