The game and local data

This driving and parking game does not require registration or login. Level progress, scores, stars, collected coins, sound and music preferences, language selection, and optional rewarded-unlock limits are stored locally. The game does not have a developer-operated gameplay server, cloud-save service, social messaging, or in-app purchases. Operating-system backups may preserve app data.

Advertising and third-party processing

The app uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). It offers optional rewarded ads to unlock the next level and may show interstitial ads at eligible level-result transitions. Ads require a network connection.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views, and crash, performance and other diagnostic information. These data support ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security and SDK reliability. Personalization depends on your consent choices, system settings, applicable law and Google’s services.

The game does not request precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, health data or payment details to play. It does not ask you to provide account credentials.

Your privacy choices

UMP updates consent information on app startup and presents a privacy message when required. Ads are requested only when the SDK permits requests. When required and available for your region and consent status, the menu or settings provides a Privacy options entry to review your choices. Watching a rewarded ad is optional; the unlock is delivered only after the SDK awards the reward and the ad closes. Declining an ad does not prevent replaying unlocked levels.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. System advertising controls and your UMP choices affect what data advertising services may use. The game does not require a Google account to play.

Sharing, security and retention

Advertising-related information is processed by Google and its advertising services. Google determines its retention periods and available deletion controls. Advertising network traffic uses transport encryption. We do not maintain a developer gameplay database or sell such a database. Local game data remain until removed, subject to system backups.

There is no developer account to delete. To remove local game data, uninstall the app and remove its app data, considering any device backups. To request deletion of data we hold, such as support correspondence, contact q908267264@gmail.com and identify the app and platform.

Audience and support

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you email support, your email address and message are used to respond to your request.

Read the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising privacy information for more information.
