1. About this game

This policy applies to Math Puzzle King-Move Matches!, provided by zhufeng Xu for iOS / App Store. Move a matchstick to solve arithmetic puzzles. The game stores progress locally, has no developer cloud save, and offers advertising and an optional purchase to remove automatic ads. We do not operate a game-account server or request your name, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise GPS location.

2. Progress and purchases

Unlocked levels, settings, purchase entitlement and review-prompt timing are stored on your device. They may be included in operating-system backups. Purchases and restoration are processed by your platform store and Unity In-App Purchasing. Product identifiers, transaction/receipt information, purchase status and technical service information may be processed to complete, validate and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card details.

3. Advertising and data use

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services may process IP address and approximate location derived from it; device, app and advertising identifiers; ad views, clicks and other app/ad interactions; advertising data; crash logs, performance and diagnostic information; and privacy choices. Google and advertising partners use these data to deliver, personalize where permitted, measure and improve advertising, operate their services and prevent fraud. Ads shown without personalization still involve data processing. The game remains playable when advertising is unavailable.

4. Tracking and your choices

On iOS, the game first shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt and then runs Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow before ads are requested. If you allow tracking, iOS makes the advertising identifier (IDFA) available to Google's advertising services. If you decline, or have not decided yet, the only effect is that the IDFA is not provided; the game does not change how it requests ads because of that choice. The game's code does not turn off ad personalization: whether ads are personalized depends on your UMP consent choices and your device settings. You can change tracking permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining does not affect gameplay. ATT permission and UMP advertising choices are separate controls; granting ATT permission does not override a UMP choice.

5. Privacy options in the game

Open Privacy from the game screen to read this policy. The game updates UMP consent information at launch and displays a required privacy message when available. Where Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Ad privacy options opens its form so you can review or change your choices. When no additional form is required, that control is unavailable and the privacy policy remains accessible. Purchasing ad removal stops automatic interstitial ads; existing service records are not automatically deleted.
