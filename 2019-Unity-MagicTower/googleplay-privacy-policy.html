Magic Tower Privacy Policy

Android · 5Game

App identifier: com.xu.magic.tower

Effective and last updated: October 11, 2026

Updated advertising practices apply to version 26.10.11 and later.

1. Scope

Magic Tower (魔塔24层：经典勇者冒险地牢) is a free offline strategy game with optional rewarded gift boxes and rewarded unlocks for additional manual save slots. There are no user accounts, chat, user-generated content, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

2. Local game data

Progress, character statistics, items, story state, unlocked save slots and preferences are stored on your device. We do not upload these saves to a developer-operated server. Removing the app or clearing its data removes local records; operating-system backups may retain separate copies.

3. Advertising and SDK data

We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). We do not use Unity Ads or a separate developer analytics service. Starting with version 26.10.11, advertising personalization and identifiers are governed by your consent, system permissions and the SDK. The app declares the Android advertising ID permission. The advertising SDK may use available advertising and other device identifiers, subject to your privacy choices and system settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. AdMob and UMP may collect and share IP address and inferred approximate location, device and network information, advertising identifiers and other device identifiers, ad views and clicks, app interactions, crash logs and performance diagnostics. These data support advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and security. Optional rewarded ads do not mean that SDK initialization processes no data. Google may link advertising information to identifiers and use it for tracking where permitted. Older versions used non-personalized ads with publisher first-party identifiers disabled and did not request ATT.

4. Permissions and choices

The game does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location permissions. Network access supports advertising and consent services. We update UMP at startup and display its required consent form where applicable. Ads initialize and load only when the SDK confirms ads may be requested; a timeout does not grant consent. Use Privacy options in the main-menu or in-game settings to review and change available privacy choices. You can play and finish the basic game without watching rewarded ads. The automatic save and first manual slot are available without ads; extra manual slots require an optional rewarded ad.

5. Third-party services

Google handles its service data according to its own privacy policies and retention rules. Processing may occur outside your country. See Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

6. Retention, deletion and rights

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app. We do not operate a cloud account or save database for this game. Advertising-service retention is controlled by the service provider. Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing and withdraw consent. Contact us with the app name, platform and request. Data controlled independently by Google may require a request to Google.

7. Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us. Store content ratings may differ by region.

8. Security and updates

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle, but cannot guarantee absolute network or storage security. We update this policy when functionality or data practices change.
