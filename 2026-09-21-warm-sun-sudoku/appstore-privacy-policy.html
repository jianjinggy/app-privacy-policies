About this policy

This policy covers Warm Sun Sudoku (暖阳数独), the offline Sudoku game provided by 余芳 伍 for iOS / iPadOS · App Store. The Android / Google Play edition has a separate policy.

Game data and local storage

Puzzle progress, notes, settings, completion history, daily-challenge state, earned in-game sunshine, rewarded-ad reward and cooldown state, and a bounded local activity log are stored on your device. This information is used to run the game, restore your progress, prevent duplicate rewards and apply ad frequency limits. You can clear the local activity log in Settings → Privacy & Data. The game has no player account, cloud save, chat, online leaderboard or in-app purchases (no real-money purchases). Puzzles are played offline on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your saved progress.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for rewarded ads and interstitial ads. Ads may be loaded before they are displayed. It also uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain applicable consent information and display privacy messages where required. Advertising availability and personalization depend on your choices, platform permissions and Google's applicable controls. Rewards are granted only after the advertising SDK reports that the reward was earned.

Information processed by Google services

Google's advertising and consent services may collect and share IP addresses and inferred approximate location; device and app information and device identifiers; app launches, taps, video views and other app/ad interactions; advertising data such as advertisements viewed; and crash, performance and other diagnostic information. These support advertising delivery, analytics and measurement, fraud prevention, security, and operation of advertising and consent services. Data can be associated with a device or app identifier even without a player account. We do not integrate a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK or additional ad networks or mediation adapters.

Identifiers and permissions

On iOS, when the game starts it first shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, then runs Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow, and only then requests ads. If you allow tracking, iOS makes the advertising identifier (IDFA) available, and Google’s advertising services may use it to deliver personalized ads and measure ad performance. If you decline, or have not decided yet, the IDFA is not provided; the game remains fully playable and continues to request ads as usual. The game’s code does not force ads to be shown without personalization: personalization follows your UMP choices, your ATT permission and Google’s controls. You can change tracking permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The game keeps Google’s publisher first-party ID enabled, so Google may also process app- or developer-scoped identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention.

The game does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise GPS location. Google estimates general location from network information rather than GPS permission. The game uses the network only for Google AdMob and UMP. Google's SDK traffic is encrypted in transit.

Your privacy choices

When required, Google displays a privacy consent form. When an advertising privacy-options entry is required by UMP, Settings → Privacy & Data shows an Ad privacy options entry so you can revisit and change your choices. Gameplay remains available when ads are unavailable, and watching rewarded ads is optional. iOS privacy settings provide additional choices. External sites or advertisers opened from an ad are governed by their own policies.

Retention, deletion and support

Local game data remains until you clear it, remove app data or uninstall, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior. Because there is no account, there is no account to delete: to delete game data, clear the local activity log in Settings → Privacy & Data, and clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove the rest. We cannot identify or delete Google's device-level advertising records through a game account because there is no such account. Google determines retention and available deletion controls for data it handles; use the privacy choices above, your device settings and Google's own controls. Uninstalling does not necessarily delete information previously received by Google. To request deletion of anything else we hold, such as support correspondence, email q908267264@gmail.com with the app name and platform. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments and use them to respond and keep necessary support records; avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Children, rights and international processing

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a regulator. Providers may process information outside your country according to their applicable safeguards. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.
