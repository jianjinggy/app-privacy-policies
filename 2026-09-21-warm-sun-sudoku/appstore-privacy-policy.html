About this policy

This policy applies to Warm Sun Sudoku (暖阳数独), an offline-first Sudoku game provided by 余芳 伍 for iPhone and iPad. The Android edition has a separate policy.

Information stored on your device

The game stores puzzle progress, notes, settings, completion history, daily-challenge state, earned in-game sunshine, ad reward and frequency-control state, and a bounded local activity log in the app sandbox. This information is used to operate the game, restore progress, prevent duplicate rewards and apply advertising frequency limits. The game does not provide an account, cloud save, chat, remote leaderboard or developer analytics server.

You can clear the local activity log from the in-game Privacy & Data screen. Clearing app data or uninstalling normally removes other local records, subject to iCloud or operating-system backup and restore behavior.

Advertising and Google services

The app uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded and interstitial ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage applicable advertising privacy choices. Depending on your region and choices, Google may process device or other identifiers, coarse location derived from network information, app interactions, advertising data, diagnostics and performance information for advertising, fraud prevention, security, measurement and service operation. Google may act as an independent service provider for information it processes.

The game disables AdMob publisher first-party ID use. After applicable UMP choices, iOS may ask for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before the advertising SDK starts. If you allow tracking, AdMob may use the advertising identifier and information from other companies' apps or websites to provide more relevant ads and measure their performance, subject to your other privacy choices. If you decline, the app does not grant tracking permission, does not request personalized ads, and you can still play normally; contextual or limited ads may remain available where permitted. You can change ATT permission in iOS Settings.

When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, it is available from Settings → Privacy & Data. Advertising failure or declining optional choices does not block normal Sudoku play. Rewarded features grant a reward only after the advertising SDK reports that the reward was earned.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Permissions and other services

The game does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise GPS location. Network access is used for AdMob and UMP. The current version has no real-money purchases and does not include a standalone analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Retention, security and international processing

Local game records remain until you clear them through available controls, clear the app's data or uninstall, subject to backup behavior. If you email us, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond and retain necessary support records. Google determines retention and deletion controls for information it handles. Service providers may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children and your choices

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a regulator. You can revisit available advertising choices in the game and use iOS settings for system-level privacy controls.

Changes

We will update this policy and its date when the app's data practices, services or contact details materially change.
