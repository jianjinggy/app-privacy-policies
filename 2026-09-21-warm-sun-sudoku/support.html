Warm Sun Sudoku · Game Support

暖阳数独 · Offline Sudoku for iPhone, iPad and Android.

How to play

Fill the 9×9 grid so that every row, column and 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once. Choose a puzzle or the daily challenge, select a cell and enter a digit, or switch to notes to pencil in candidates. Progress, notes and settings are stored locally on your device.

Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove progress. Rewarded ads are optional and depend on ad availability; if no ad is available, you can keep playing. You can clear the local activity log and, when required by Google, open Ad privacy options in Settings → Privacy & Data.
