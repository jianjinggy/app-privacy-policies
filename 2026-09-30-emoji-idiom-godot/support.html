Email q908267264@gmail.com. Please include your device model, operating-system version, app version, puzzle theme and level, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot is optional; remove personal information before sending it.

How do I play?

Use the emoji clues to choose answer characters in order. The first level in each theme includes an interactive guide whenever you replay it. Incorrect answers can be cleared and tried again.

Why is a hint ad unavailable?

Hint ads require a working connection and an available ad. Consent settings and advertising-provider availability can affect loading. If an ad is not ready, continue playing or try again later. Canceling an ad or closing it before earning its reward does not grant an advertising reward.

How can I change audio or language?

Open Settings to adjust music and sound-effect volumes independently, or switch between Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

Where is progress saved?

Progress and preferences are saved locally. There is no game account or cloud synchronization. We cannot restore a lost local save from a developer server. Your device may separately support operating-system backups.

How do I reset or remove data?

Reset Progress removes game progress, coins, favorites, and daily challenge history; some preferences and review-request records remain. To remove all local data, use your operating system's app-data controls or uninstall the app. Review system backup settings if you want to prevent an old save from being restored.

Privacy and advertising choices

UMP privacy options appear in Settings when required. On iOS, tracking permission can also be changed in system settings. On Android, advertising-ID controls are available in system settings, depending on the operating-system version. See the platform-specific policy below for details.

Support privacy

We use the address and information you send by email to answer your request. Do not include passwords, credentials, payment details, or unnecessary personal information.
