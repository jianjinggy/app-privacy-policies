Box Math: Picture Equations is a family-friendly educational math game. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Everyday Game Studio does not collect or receive personal information from this app. The app does not collect or transmit:

The app stores only the information needed to provide its game features on the user's device, such as game progress, settings, and the local state of an unlocked purchase. The app receives only the purchase result and product information needed to unlock or restore the purchased content. Google may process account, payment, and purchase information under its own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Box Math: Picture Equations contains no advertisements. The app does not use advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting services, or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not track users across apps or websites and do not use the Android Advertising ID for advertising or measurement.

Because Everyday Game Studio does not collect personal information from the app, it does not sell, rent, or share such information and has no personal information to retain. Local game data remains on the device until the user removes it. Google Play purchase records are handled by Google under Google's policies.
