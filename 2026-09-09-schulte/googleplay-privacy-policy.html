iOS / iPadOS policy / 另一平台政策

1. About this app / 适用范围

This policy applies to Schulte Training (舒尔特训练 / 舒尔特方格训练), provided by Everyday Game Studio, with application identifier com.xuzengfei.schulte.y2026. It covers this new application only, not earlier Schulte applications. The app offers local attention, memory and reaction training, challenges, local two-player activities and training history. It has no accounts, cloud score synchronization, purchases, chat or standalone analytics SDK.

本政策适用于 Everyday Game Studio 提供的新应用“舒尔特训练 / 舒尔特方格训练”，应用标识为 com.xuzengfei.schulte.y2026，不适用于旧舒尔特应用。本应用提供本地专注力、记忆力及反应训练、挑战、同设备双人活动和历史成绩，无账号、云端成绩同步、内购、聊天或独立统计 SDK。

2. Local information / 本地信息

Training results, difficulty and challenge progress, unfinished puzzle drafts, language and sound settings, completed-round counts and the most recent ad-display timestamp are stored locally on your device. App code does not upload training records to a developer server. The app does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

训练成绩、难度和挑战进度、未完成题目草稿、语言与音效设置、完成局数及最近广告展示时间保存在设备本地。应用代码不向开发者服务器上传训练记录，不请求通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风或精确位置权限。

3. Advertising and service data / 广告及服务数据

The app integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and User Messaging Platform (UMP). It requests non-personalized interstitial ads at occasional breaks after training. The SDK may process IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad views and interactions, crash logs, performance information, consent choices and technical identifiers permitted by the configuration below. Google and its advertising service providers process these data to deliver and measure ads, operate services and prevent fraud. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is processed. Training remains usable when an ad is unavailable.

应用接入 Google AdMob（Google 移动广告 SDK）与 UMP，在训练结束后的部分间隙请求非个性化插屏广告。SDK 可能处理 IP 地址及据此估算的大致位置、设备与应用信息、广告展示和交互、崩溃日志、性能数据、隐私选择及下述配置允许的技术标识符。Google 及其广告服务提供商为投放和衡量广告、运营服务和防止欺诈处理这些数据。非个性化广告不等于不处理数据；没有可用广告时仍可正常训练。

4. Device advertising identifiers / 设备广告标识符

The app removes the Android advertising ID (AD_ID) permission and the AdServices advertising ID, attribution and topics permissions from its manifest. Advertising is enabled only on Android 13 or later, where the AD_ID permission is enforced; older supported systems can run training without ads. Publisher first-party ID is disabled. Google may still process app set ID and other SDK technical identifiers for its permitted services.

应用已移除 Android 广告 ID（AD_ID）权限及 AdServices 广告 ID、归因和主题权限。仅在强制检查 AD_ID 权限的 Android 13 及以上启用广告；较旧的受支持系统仍可无广告训练。发布商第一方广告 ID 已关闭。Google 仍可能为其服务处理应用集 ID 等 SDK 技术标识符。

5. Privacy choices and timeout / 隐私选择与超时

The app updates UMP consent information at launch and presents a required consent form when it can do so without interrupting training. A 10-second loading deadline does not grant consent. After a timeout or error, an ad can be requested only when UMP reports canRequestAds=true, including where a valid previous choice is available. Otherwise training continues without ads. A form already displayed is not dismissed by the loading deadline. Where required, Settings → Privacy choices opens Google’s privacy options so you can review or change your choice. Settings also links to this policy.

应用启动时更新 UMP 同意信息，并在不打断训练时展示所需表单。加载等待上限为 10 秒；超时不代表同意。超时或出错后，仅在 UMP 返回 canRequestAds=true（包括存在有效的历史选择）时才请求广告，否则继续无广告训练。已经展示的表单不会被加载超时自动关闭。需要时，可通过“设置 → 隐私选项”查看或更改 Google 隐私选择；设置也提供本政策链接。

6. Retention, deletion and requests / 保存、删除与权利

Local app records remain until removed using operating-system app-data controls or uninstalling the app, subject to device backup and restore settings. We do not operate a server account for your training data. Google determines retention for data it controls according to its policies. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent or complain to a supervisory authority. Contact us about developer-controlled data; requests about Google-controlled data may also need to be sent to Google. Google services may process data outside your country under their applicable safeguards.

本地记录保留至通过系统应用数据管理或卸载应用删除，设备备份和恢复设置可能影响删除结果。我们不为训练数据提供服务器账号。Google 根据其政策决定其控制数据的保留时间。根据所在地区，您可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制或反对处理、撤回同意以及向监管机构投诉的权利。涉及开发者控制的数据请联系我们；涉及 Google 控制的数据也可能需要向 Google 提交请求。Google 服务可能根据其适用保障措施在您所在国家或地区之外处理数据。

The app does not ask users to submit personal information to the developer to train. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information, please contact us. We will review the concern and take appropriate action. Network services and device storage cannot be guaranteed completely secure. If data practices change, we will update this policy and its date.

应用不要求用户向开发者提交个人信息才能训练。如家长或监护人认为儿童提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会核查并采取适当措施。网络服务和设备存储无法保证绝对安全。数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策及日期。
