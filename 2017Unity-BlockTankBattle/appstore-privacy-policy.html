Tank Battle 1990-Classic Game
Apple App Store · iPhone and iPad

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled in the iOS game Tank Battle 1990-Classic Game (App Store ID 1247512689; bundle identifier com.zhu.tank.battle), including its advertising services.

Game progress and settings

You can play without creating an account. Level progress, best star ratings and game settings are stored on your device. We do not operate an account service or a server that stores your saved battles. Removing the app may remove its local progress; device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

This update does not add Firebase Analytics or a separate player-behavior analytics service. We use reports supplied by Apple and our advertising providers to understand downloads and advertising performance. Advertising SDKs still process data as described below.

Advertising and associated data

The game uses Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These providers may process:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device and operating-system information, and app version.
  • App- or device-related identifiers. Access to Apple's advertising identifier is subject to Apple's permission controls.
  • Ad requests, impressions, video views, ad interactions and related usage information.
  • Crash, performance and diagnostic information.

Providers use this information to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, diagnose technical problems and improve their services. Their data processing, retention and international transfers are described in their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily stop all advertising or all data processing needed to deliver, secure and measure ads. Watching a rewarded ad is optional; free retry remains available in the game.

Your privacy choices

Where applicable, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present advertising privacy choices. When a privacy-options entry is required, you can reopen it from the game's home screen to review or change your choices. Available options depend on your region and the advertising provider.

You can also manage applicable app permissions in iOS Settings. A choice about advertising does not grant permission to access your contacts, photos, microphone or precise GPS location; the game does not request those permissions for gameplay.

Contact, retention and requests

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Advertising data is handled under the providers' retention practices. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing. Children

The game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address it.
