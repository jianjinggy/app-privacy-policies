What the game stores

Your solved levels, unlocked game modes, tutorial state, sound preferences, and local ad-pacing timestamps are saved only on your device. The game has no user accounts, cloud save, or developer-operated gameplay server. It has no in-app purchases and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location.

Advertising and consent

We use Google AdMob for interstitial ads and optional rewarded video ads (to view an answer hint or unlock additional puzzle modes early). As described in Google’s SDK documentation, Google may collect and share information such as IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or other identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), ad views and other app interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics. These are used to serve and measure advertising, detect fraud and abuse, and maintain the service. No separate third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or ad-mediation SDKs are bundled.

Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents consent choices where required. When available in your region, open Settings inside the game and select Ad privacy options to review or update your choices. Android also provides system controls to reset or delete the Advertising ID on supported devices. Declining optional ads does not prevent you from playing the game.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps.

Support, retention, and deletion

If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply and resolve your inquiry. You can ask us to access or delete support correspondence we hold by contacting the address below. Google controls retention and privacy choices for data processed by its SDK. To delete your local game save, clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app; system backups depend on your device settings.

Children and privacy rights

This puzzle game is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and older) and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can delete it. Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data.
