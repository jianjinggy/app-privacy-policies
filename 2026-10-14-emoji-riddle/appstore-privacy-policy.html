Policy for the other platform

1. The game and local records

This policy covers this picture-and-word puzzle game, provided by 余芳 伍. The game has no player registration, developer-operated cloud save, chat, online leaderboard or real-money purchases. Puzzle progress and ordinary puzzle play are available locally. Advertising requires an internet connection.

Cleared puzzle identifiers, unlocked levels, language and sound preferences, and advertising frequency and reward state are stored on your device. The game does not upload these saves to a developer-operated server. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise GPS location. Connection checks before advertisements inspect local network availability.

2. Advertising and consent services

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). It offers optional rewarded advertisements for a one-character hint and occasional interstitial advertisements after a completed puzzle, with frequency limits. It does not integrate additional advertising networks or a separate analytics SDK.

Each hint first offers Cancel and Watch Ad. A hint is delivered only after the SDK reports that the reward was earned and the advertisement closes. An unavailable ad, declined invitation or early closure without a reward does not give a hint. You can continue answering puzzles without using hints. Interstitials are not placed in the middle of answering a puzzle.

3. Information processed by Google

Advertising and consent services may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it; advertising, device and app identifiers; ad impressions, taps and video-view events; and crash, performance and diagnostic information. Consent preferences and information needed to display and operate consent forms may also be processed. Depending on the data type and your choices, information may be associated with a device or app identifier.

Google and its service providers use this information to deliver and measure advertisements, analyze service performance, manage privacy choices, protect security and prevent fraud. Personalized advertising depends on applicable permission, consent and Google controls. Advertising without personalization may still process information. Google SDK network transmissions use TLS encryption; internet services and device storage cannot be guaranteed completely secure.

For Google-controlled processing, see Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Google may process information outside your country under its applicable arrangements.

4. Your permission and advertising choices

On iOS and iPadOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) request precedes Google’s consent update. Tracking and access to the advertising identifier (IDFA) depend on your system permission. Declining ATT does not prevent puzzle play and does not bypass other applicable consent requirements. You can review tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google may also use permitted app- or developer-scoped identifiers.

The game updates UMP consent information at launch and shows a consent form when required. Ad initialization and requests are permitted only when the native consent service allows them. The Settings screen provides Privacy Options when required by UMP so you can review or change your choice, and always links to this policy. Denying consent does not lock the puzzle game.

5. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until app data is removed or the app is uninstalled, subject to operating-system backup and restoration behavior. There is no game account to delete. Removing the app does not necessarily delete data already received by Google. Google determines retention for the information it controls; use its privacy controls or contact Google about that information.

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and keep only the records needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Email us to request deletion of information we hold, identifying the game and platform. Do not include passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a supervisory authority. Contact us for developer-controlled information; Google-controlled information is also subject to Google’s request procedures.

The game does not ask players to enter a name, birth date or other personal details. Parents and guardians can contact us about a child’s privacy, and we will review the concern. If the game’s data practices or contact details change, we will update this page and its date.
