iOS / iPadOS policy

1. About this app

This policy applies to Matchstick Master, a single-player matchstick puzzle game provided by Everyday Game Studio. The game provides local puzzles, progress tracking, language selection and sound settings. It does not provide accounts, chat, user-generated content, subscriptions, in-app purchases or payment processing.

2. Information stored locally

The game stores completed-puzzle progress, skipped-puzzle progress where carried over from an earlier save, the selected language and the sound preference in local app storage. These records are used to resume gameplay and apply settings. The game does not upload them to an Everyday Game Studio server. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes local records, subject to operating-system backup behavior.

3. Non-personalized advertising and basic device information

The Android version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for optional, user-initiated rewarded ads that can reveal a puzzle hint. It does not request interstitial or automatic background ads. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized (including the npa=1 request setting), and the game has no standalone analytics SDK.

Google and its advertising service providers may process information needed to request, deliver, measure, secure and limit the ads. This may include IP address and an approximate location inferred from it, device and app information, operating-system and network details, ad requests, impressions and interactions, consent choices, diagnostic or performance information, and technical identifiers permitted by the applicable configuration. Non-personalized advertising can still involve this basic device and service processing; it does not mean that no data is processed.

4. Android permissions and privacy choices

The Android build declares network access and network-state access for advertising and related services. The game does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth or location permissions. It does not require a name, email address, account or payment details to play.

Where Google requires it, UMP can present a consent form and provide privacy choices. The Settings screen also includes an Ad privacy action when the platform makes Google's privacy-options form available. The Settings screen's Privacy Policy button opens this page in the device's default browser. You can decline to watch a rewarded ad and continue playing, but the associated hint is not revealed.

5. Support, deletion and requests

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so that we can respond to support or privacy requests. We retain support correspondence only as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. To request deletion of information controlled by us, email the address below with the app name, platform and details of the request. Information controlled independently by Google may need to be managed through Google's privacy controls.

6. Children, security and changes

The game does not knowingly ask children or adults to submit personal information directly to the developer. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us so that we can review the request. We use reasonable measures for the limited information handled by the game and rely on Google and Android security controls for their services, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. We will update this policy if the game's data practices, services or contact details change.

7. Your rights and third-party privacy information

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent where applicable. Contact us with the app name, platform, country or region and the nature of the request. Requests about information controlled by Google may also need to be sent to Google.

See Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps and Google Ads Settings.
