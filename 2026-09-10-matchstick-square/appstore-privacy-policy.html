Android policy

1. About this app

This policy applies to Matchstick Master, a single-player matchstick puzzle game provided by Everyday Game Studio. The game provides local puzzles, progress tracking, language selection and sound settings. It does not provide accounts, chat, user-generated content, subscriptions, in-app purchases or payment processing.

2. Information stored locally

The game stores completed-puzzle progress, skipped-puzzle progress where carried over from an earlier save, the selected language and the sound preference in local app storage. These records are used to resume gameplay and apply settings. The game does not upload them to an Everyday Game Studio server. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes local records, subject to operating-system backup behavior.

3. Advertising and basic device information

The current iOS/iPadOS configuration does not enable the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) or User Messaging Platform (UMP) integration, so it does not request or display ads and does not use personalized advertising. The Android version uses optional, user-initiated rewarded ads for puzzle hints, and those requests are configured as non-personalized. If an iOS release enables advertising or another data-using service, we will update this page before or with that release.

When the Android advertising service is used, Google and its service providers may process basic device and service information such as IP address and an approximate location inferred from it, device and app information, operating-system and network details, ad requests and interactions, consent choices, diagnostic or performance information, and technical identifiers permitted by the applicable configuration. Non-personalized advertising can still involve this technical processing; it does not mean that no data is processed.

4. iOS/iPadOS permissions and privacy choices

The current iOS/iPadOS configuration does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth or precise-location access. It does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization or access the IDFA. Gameplay can be used without an account or network service.

Settings includes a Privacy Policy button that opens this page in the device's default browser. UMP privacy choices are available only on builds where UMP is enabled; the current iOS/iPadOS configuration does not show an advertising consent form.

5. Support, deletion and requests

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so that we can respond to support or privacy requests. We retain support correspondence only as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. To request deletion of information controlled by us, email the address below with the app name, platform and details of the request. Information controlled independently by Google or Apple may need to be managed through their privacy controls.

6. Children, security and changes

The game does not knowingly ask children or adults to submit personal information directly to the developer. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us so that we can review the request. We use reasonable measures for the limited information handled by the game, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. We will update this policy if the game's data practices, services or contact details change.

7. Third-party privacy information

For information about services that may be used by a future or platform-specific release, see Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps and Apple's Privacy Policy.
