Game data on your device

The game stores coins, aircraft and upgrades, unlocked stages, medals, best scores, settings, language preference, tutorial progress, rewarded-ad limits and advertising frequency state locally. This supports play, progress restoration and prevention of duplicate rewards. We do not operate a server that receives these game saves. The game has no player account, cloud save, chat, online leaderboard or real-money in-app purchases. Gameplay is available without an internet connection; advertising needs a connection.

Optional advertising

The game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). This development build uses Google's official test advertisements; the SDK can still process device and network data. Only rewarded ads and rewarded interstitial ads are enabled. Rewarded interstitials at eligible game-end screens explain the 30-coin reward and provide a Cancel option before playback. Coins are granted only after the SDK confirms eligibility and the ad closes. There are no banner, app-open or native-ad placements, additional ad networks or separate analytics SDKs.

Rewarded advertisements are voluntary: the game first explains the reward and offers Cancel and Watch. The normal ad reward is delivered after the SDK reports that it was earned and the advertisement closes. Early closure without a reward callback gives no ad reward. If an advertisement genuinely fails while a real network is available, the game may explicitly offer a fallback bonus after its waiting period; that bonus is distinguished from an earned advertisement. Interstitials are limited to existing game-ending screens with frequency limits and are excluded from the tutorial stage.

Data processed by Google

Google's advertising and consent services may process IP addresses and inferred approximate location; device, app and advertising identifiers; advertisements viewed and interactions such as taps and video views; and crash, performance and diagnostic data. These support advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security and service operation. Such information can relate to a device or app identifier without a player account. Google's services may process data outside your country. Google's SDK requests are encrypted in transit.

Permissions and privacy choices

On Android, the game updates UMP consent information on every launch. Only the native UMP canRequestAds result allows ad initialization or requests. The advertising SDK may use the Android advertising ID, app- or developer-scoped identifiers and applicable advertising services as permitted by your device and consent choices. The game preserves the advertising permissions required by the SDK. You can use the advertising privacy and advertising-ID controls available in your Android device Settings; the exact options depend on the OS version.

The Settings screen includes a Privacy Policy link and, whenever UMP requires it, a Privacy Options button that opens Google's consent-management form. Refusing tracking or consent does not block the game. We do not request contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise GPS permission. Network-interface checks used before advertisements examine connection availability locally; they are not a test of whether a Google website can be reached.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Their controls and retention practices apply to data handled by Google.

Retention and deletion

Local game saves remain until you clear them, remove app data or uninstall, subject to the operating system's backup and restore behavior. Settings → Clear Data resets game progress, coins, medals, upgrades and tips; advertising cooldown or daily-limit information can remain until app data is removed or the app is uninstalled. Uninstalling does not necessarily erase data already received by Google. There is no game account to delete. Use Google's controls for data it handles, and contact us for deletion requests relating to information we hold.

Support and children

The game does not ask players to enter a name, birth date or other personal details. Parents and guardians may contact us about a child's privacy. If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond and retain necessary support records. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.
