Privacy Policy — 火眼数数 for Android

Effective and updated: October 12, 2026 · Everyday Game Studio

This policy describes the Android version of 火眼数数. It covers information handled locally by the game and information handled by its advertising and consent providers.

Local game information

The game stores level progress, earned stars, best completion times, the timed-challenge high score, language, music and sound-effect volumes, a per-version review-request marker, and the timing of the most recent rewarded ad on your device. These records support gameplay and advertising cooldowns. They are not uploaded to a server operated by Everyday Game Studio. The game has no account-registration, sign-in, cloud-save or multiplayer service, and does not offer in-app purchases.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for optional rewarded ads that provide a counting hint and interstitial ads at completed-level or challenge screens. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages applicable consent messages and privacy choices. Requests are gated by the SDK's consent state. Cancelling a rewarded-ad invitation does not award a hint. Tutorials do not show rewarded or interstitial ads.

Depending on device settings, consent and the ad served, Google may process IP addresses (including an estimate of general location), advertising and other device identifiers, ad impressions, ad/video interactions, app interactions, and crash, performance or other diagnostic information. Google uses this information for delivering and measuring ads, analytics related to advertising, and preventing fraud or abuse. UMP may process general location, performance information and consent-form interactions to provide consent functionality, and stores privacy preferences through the platform. The game does not include a separate Firebase Analytics service or send its local level-save file to Google as an analytics feed.

Google operates its services under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Your Android privacy choices

Manage or delete/reset your advertising ID in Android's advertising/privacy settings; availability and wording depend on the Android version. The SDK may use advertising ID and app-set ID subject to platform and consent rules. Internet and network-state access support advertising and local connection checks. The game does not request precise-location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo-library access for gameplay.

When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings includes Privacy Options to review or change applicable choices. You can decline an optional rewarded-ad invitation. Your device and Google may offer additional privacy controls; consent choices and tracking permission are distinct.

Information you choose to send

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that correspondence to respond and resolve your request. You do not need to send your game-save file, advertising identifier or sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Removing the app and its app data removes its local save from the device; operating-system backups are managed by the platform. We do not hold a cloud copy of your game progress. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any legal retention obligation. Google determines retention and deletion for information it processes; the local reset does not delete Google's service records.

Security and international processing

Google states that Mobile Ads SDK transmissions use TLS. The local save is stored in the app's device storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Google and email-service providers may process information in countries other than your own, under their respective policies.

Children and parents

The game does not ask players to enter their age or date of birth. Parents and guardians should review the store's age rating and the game's advertising and privacy choices before use by a child. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information to our support address.
