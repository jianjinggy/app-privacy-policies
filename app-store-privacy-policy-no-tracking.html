Box Math: Picture Equations is an educational math game. The app does not require an account and does not collect personal information. It does not contain advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information through the app. In particular, the app does not collect:

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores limited game information locally on your device, such as level progress, settings, and whether an in-app purchase has unlocked the full game. This information is used only to provide the app's features. It is not sent to us and is not linked to an identity or account.

You can remove this local information by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data using your device settings. The app does not provide cloud synchronization.

The app offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock the full game. Payments are processed by Google Play Billing or Apple StoreKit, depending on the platform. We do not receive or store your complete payment card number, bank details, or store account password.

The app receives only the purchase result and product information needed to grant or restore the purchased unlock. Google and Apple may process purchase, account, and payment information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Advertising and Tracking

Box Math: Picture Equations contains no advertisements. We do not use advertising networks, analytics services, or other third-party tracking SDKs. We do not track users across apps or websites, do not use IDFA or AAID for advertising or measurement, and do not request permission for tracking.

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not share personal information with third parties and have no personal information to retain.
