Arrow Maze Privacy Policy

Platform: Android and Google Play

Effective date: August 27, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Arrow Maze (“Arrow Maze,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the game.

1. Information stored on your device

Arrow Maze stores gameplay and preference information locally on your device so the game can remember your experience. This may include your language choice, sound and music settings, current and unlocked levels, star results, and in-game hint or grid counts.

This local game data is not sent to our servers. The app does not require you to create an account and does not provide developer-operated cloud sync. You can use the Privacy Center in the app to clear local game data. Removing the app or clearing its app data also removes locally stored game data, subject to Android’s normal behavior.

2. Advertising

Arrow Maze is free and uses advertising, including rewarded and interstitial advertising. The Android app uses Google Mobile Ads to request non-personalized ads. It does not use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or provide an in-app advertising consent form.

To serve non-personalized ads and support ad delivery, measurement, frequency limits, and fraud prevention, Google and its advertising partners may process technical information such as device and app information, IP address or approximate location inferred from it, the Android Advertising ID or similar device identifiers, ad requests and interactions, and diagnostics. The app does not request personalized advertising.

Google’s processing is governed by Google’s own policies and terms. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Information you send to us

If you email us for support, we receive the email address, message, attachments, and other information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request, troubleshoot the app, and keep appropriate support records.

We do not provide an in-game social profile, user account, user-generated content service, or in-app purchase system.

4. Information we do not intentionally collect through the game

The core game does not need access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location. We also do not include a separate developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service in the app. Advertising providers may still make their own network requests and process the information described in Section 2.

5. Retention, security, and international processing

Local game data remains on your device until you clear it, remove the app, or Android removes it. Advertising providers retain information according to their policies and operational, legal, security, and fraud-prevention needs. Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and maintain necessary records.

Google and other service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. We use the platform and service-provider controls available to the app, but no method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

6. Your choices and requests

  • Use the in-app Privacy Center to open the policy and clear local game data.
  • Use the privacy and advertising controls available in Android. The game itself does not provide an in-app advertising consent form.
  • Contact us about a privacy question or a request concerning information you sent directly to us. Because gameplay data is stored locally and no Arrow Maze account or game server is used, we generally cannot retrieve or delete that local data remotely; clear it in the app or remove the app instead.

7. Children

Arrow Maze does not knowingly ask children to submit personal information to us. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Advertising shown to users is also subject to the platform and advertising provider requirements of Google Play and the advertising provider.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, advertising services, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the “Last updated” date. Your continued use of Arrow Maze after an update means that you have read the revised policy.
