What to include in a support request

The following details help us investigate more quickly:

  • iPhone or iPad model and iOS or iPadOS version
  • App version and the language selected in the app
  • The level or screen where the issue occurred
  • Steps to reproduce the issue and what you expected to happen
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if it does not contain private information

Please do not send passwords, payment information, advertising identifiers, or other sensitive data.

Common help topics

Progress and settings

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. The game does not require an account or developer-operated cloud sync. If you use the in-app Privacy Center to clear local data, locally stored progress and settings may be removed from that device.

Ads and privacy choices

Arrow Maze is free and includes ads. Depending on your device and region, Apple tracking and consent choices can affect how ads are served. You can review available advertising privacy options from the in-app Privacy Center.
